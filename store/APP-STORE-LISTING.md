## Description (4000 max)
```
Shapester is a fast, retro puzzle game about packing.

Every map drops 15 random geometric shapes on the board. A yellow box wraps around all of them — your job is to make that box as small as possible. Drag pieces with one finger, move and rotate them with two, and push them against each other: shapes collide and pivot like real objects, so you can nudge them into tight spots.

PLAY ONLINE WITH FRIENDS
• Create a room or join one from the lobby — 2 to 6 players.
• Every round lasts 15 seconds and everyone gets exactly the same map.
• Smallest area wins: each round you score a point for every player you beat.
• 5 rounds per match, then the winner is crowned.

PRACTICE
• Play solo, generate as many new maps as you want and chase your best fill percentage.

FEATURES
• Real physics: shapes slide and pivot when they collide.
• Triangles, rectangles, trapezoids, polygons — every map is different.
• Chunky pixel-art look with CRT scanlines.
• No ads, no accounts, no tracking.

How tight can you pack it?
```
