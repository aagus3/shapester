Shapester – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Shapester ("the app") is a puzzle game developed by Agustin Ferrante. This policy explains what information the app handles. In short: no accounts, no ads, no tracking, no purchases.

Information the app uses

  • Anonymous player ID. To play online, the app signs in anonymously with Unity Gaming Services (Unity Authentication). This creates a random identifier that is not linked to your name, email or any other personal information. It is used only to make online play work.
  • Nickname. You can choose a nickname of up to 10 characters (a random one is generated by default). It is stored on your device and shown to the other players in your room. Please don't use your real name or personal information as a nickname.
  • Network connection data. Online matches are connected through Unity's Relay and Lobby services, which necessarily process technical data such as your IP address to deliver game messages between players. This data is handled by Unity Technologies according to the Unity Privacy Policy.
  • Game results. During an online match, the area you achieve each round and your score are shared with the other players in the same room. They are not stored after the match ends.

What the app does NOT do

  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No analytics, no in-app purchases, no location, contacts, camera or microphone access.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data.

Practice mode

Practice mode works entirely on your device and does not send any data.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The only user-provided text is the optional nickname described above.

Data deletion

Deleting the app removes the nickname and the anonymous identifier stored on your device. If you want us to request deletion of the anonymous identifier from Unity's services, contact us at the address below.
